FOUNDER'S JOURNEY • SANA

THE STORY BEHIND NUTIYALA

Nutiyala is more than just a clothing store — it is the beginning of a 15-year-old dream.

I’m Sana, the founder of Nutiyala. As a Muslim girl, stepping beyond the comfort of home, facing life’s challenges and family circumstances, and building something of my own was never an easy journey. But every challenge taught me to be stronger, and every setback brought me one step closer to this dream. Today, Nutiyala is not the destination. It is only the beginning — the first step towards a dream I have carried in my heart for 15 years.

What makes Nutiyala special is simple: every piece is made for you, and only you. Every design, every detail and every customization is created with one person in mind the person who will wear it. Because at Nutiyala, we believe clothing should feel personal, not ordinary.

Behind this journey is a family who stood by me with love, faith and support. their encouragement gave me the strength to keep going when things were not easy. Through Nutiyala, I hope to create more than beautiful clothes. I want to inspire every girl, especially Muslim girls, to believe that their dreams are possible, no matter where they begin.

This is Sana’s dream. This is her beginning. This is Nutiyala — Made Just For You.

THE MAKING OF A PIECE

From the first stitch
to the final detail.

Every garment is born at the intersection of artisanal precision and poetic silhouette. Over 40 hours of hand zardozi stitching, pure botanical dyeing, and hand-finished hems.

01

Thread Selection

Pure zari woven with 24k gold bullion leaf and high-twist Mulberry silk threads.

02

Artisanal Looming

Heritage pit-looms guided by generational master weavers in Varanasi and Chanderi.

03

Zardozi Needlework

Hand-guided needle embroidery placing intricate dabka, metallic coils, and seed pearls.

04

Bespoke Atelier Finishing

Hand-scalloped hems, steam-sculpted drape, and sealed with our master artisan's hallmark.
